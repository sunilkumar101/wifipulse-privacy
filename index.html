WiFi Pulse 6E/7 – Privacy Policy

Effective 21 October 2026 · App ID com.wifipulse.analyzer

WiFi Pulse 6E/7 ("the app") is a Wi-Fi analyzer. It measures your Wi-Fi connection, nearby networks and devices on your own network, and shows the results on your phone. We (the developer) do not run any servers and do not collect, receive or sell your personal data. The only third parties that receive data are Google's advertising and payment services, described below.

1. Data that stays on your phone

Everything the app measures is processed and stored only on your device, in the app's private storage:

  • Your Wi-Fi connection details (network name, BSSID, signal strength, channel, link speed, IP and gateway address).
  • Nearby Wi-Fi networks from Android's Wi-Fi scan (names, BSSIDs, signal, channel, security type).
  • Devices found on your own local network (IP address, name they announce, device type) and any names or "trusted" marks you add.
  • Heatmap surveys: floor plans you draw, signal samples you record, and a floor-plan photo if you choose to import one.
  • Speed and ping test results while the screen is open.

This data is never uploaded by the app. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Files you export (CSV, JSON, PDF) are saved only where you choose, and shared only if you share them.

3. Network tests

When you start a test, the app connects to these public services. They see your public IP address, as with any internet connection, but no other data from the app is sent:

  • Speed test: speed.cloudflare.com (Cloudflare).
  • Gamer ping: Amazon Web Services regional endpoints (dynamodb.<region>.amazonaws.com), www.youtube.com, www.twitch.tv.
  • DNS test: Cloudflare (1.1.1.1), Google (8.8.8.8) and Quad9 (9.9.9.9) DNS resolvers.

Device discovery and the router admin page only talk to devices on your own local network.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and ad interactions to show and measure ads, and for fraud prevention. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent first using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Buying WiFi Pulse Pro removes all ads.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time WiFi Pulse Pro purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that Pro was bought, so it can turn ads off.

6. Children

The app is a technical tool for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Security

Data stays in Android's private app storage, which other apps cannot read. The router admin page is opened inside the app only for your own gateway address; the app never accepts invalid HTTPS certificates silently.
